Two ways to partner

Option A — recommended

Joint venture · NewCo, minimum 50/50

  • Ownership: NewCo formed between our company and the lender, minimum 50/50 on the cap table.
  • Exit protection: mutual right of first refusal — either partner may buy the other out at fair market value before any outside sale.
  • Lead flow: leads are routed only where the lender has licensed availability and closer capacity.
  • Execution: the lender's team works and closes 100% of every deal.
  • Add-on revenue split 50/50 on all leads we send: insurance, real estate, title, and hard money.
  • Replaces the monthly sponsorship fee entirely.

How the volume is produced

Traffic is not bought on the open market. It is generated and qualified by three proprietary systems we own and operate.

R0cketShip

r0cketship.com

The distribution engine. Builds and operates a nationwide network of white-label, search-optimized properties across forty-plus niches, then delivers ZIP-exclusive leads straight into the partner's CRM. Owns the top-of-funnel inventory so acquisition cost is controlled rather than bid on.

Predictive Data

predictivedata.org

The demand engine. Scores and targets audiences on intent signals before a form is ever filled, so spend concentrates on the households statistically closest to a mortgage decision. This is what compresses 10,000–13,000 users into 1,000 funded loans instead of a far larger, far colder pool.

KeywordCalls

keywordcalls.com

The conversion layer. High-intent prospects are delivered as live inbound phone calls, in real time, keyword by keyword, routed to the closer's line. A ringing phone from a self-selected searcher converts at a different rate than a submitted form.
