The flow — inbound call to issued policy, without a human in the loop

1Inbound

Customer calls. Answered on the first ring, at any hour, at any volume.

▶
2Intake

The voice engine asks three questions: age, state, and the monthly premium they have in mind.

▶
3Rate

Answers hit the carrier's price-sheet grid in real time. State-aware, age-banded.

▶
4Quote

Coverage and premium spoken back inside the same conversation. No callback, no "an agent will reach out."

▶
5Decision

"Any questions, or would you like to enroll?" Questions loop back to the answer engine and return here.

▶
6Text sent

A secure enrollment link goes to the customer's mobile while they are still on the line.

7Wait for reply

The agent holds the call and waits for the customer to open the link and confirm. Nobody is transferred.

▶
8Payment

The customer pays through the third-party administrator. Card data never touches the agent or the call.

▶
9Post-back

The administrator posts acceptance back to the engine. Policy is issued and documents are delivered.

▶
10Confirm

"Thank you — you're covered." Confirmation is spoken and sent in writing.

▶
11Stay on

The agent does not hang up: "Is there anything else I can help you with today?"

▶
12Next need

Any second need re-enters intake at step 2. One call, many policies.

↺ CONTINUOUS The call never dead-ends. Questions return to the quote. A completed enrollment returns to intake. The only way the conversation closes is the customer saying they are finished.

Why the AI wins this job

It mitigates risk

Every conversation follows the same approved script, quotes only carrier-filed rates, and is captured end to end. No off-script statements, no promises the policy does not make, no bad day. Compliance becomes a property of the system rather than a training problem.

It is always on

Nights, weekends, holidays and the hour after a news story about an accident. Call volume that would need a fifty-seat floor is handled concurrently, and the fiftieth caller gets the same conversation as the first.

It has no employment overhead

No salary, benefits, holiday, sick pay, overtime, recruiting, ramp time, attrition or seat cost. Capacity scales with demand instead of with headcount, and a slow month costs nothing.

It streamlines the process

Three questions to a quote. One text to enroll. One post-back to issue. What used to be a callback, a paper application and a chase becomes a single unbroken conversation.

A win on both sides of the transaction

Risk mitigation, cost savings and a shorter path to coverage — for the person buying and the carrier writing.

For the consumerAnswered instantly, quoted honestly against their own budget, covered before they hang up.
For the carrierLower acquisition cost, consistent disclosure, cleaner applications and an auditable record of every word.
