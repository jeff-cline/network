Phase 3

The money

8

Payment runs through the third-party administrator

The customer pays the administrator directly. Card details never touch the agent, the call, or the recording — which is exactly where you want that boundary.

9

The administrator posts acceptance back

Acceptance returns to the engine automatically. The policy is issued and the documents go out — all before the call ends.

Why AI

Why the machine is right for this job

🛡️

It mitigates risk

The same approved script every time. Only carrier-filed rates. No off-script statements, no promises the policy doesn't make, no bad day. Every word captured and auditable. Compliance stops being a training problem and becomes a property of the system.

🌙

It is always on

Nights, weekends, holidays, and the hour after a news story sends people looking for accident cover. Volume that would need a fifty-seat floor is handled at once — and the fiftieth caller gets the same conversation as the first.

💸

It carries no employment overhead

No salary, benefits, holiday, sick pay, overtime, recruiting, ramp time, attrition or seat cost. Capacity scales with demand rather than headcount, and a slow month costs nothing at all.

⚡

It streamlines everything

Three questions to a quote. One text to enroll. One post-back to issue. What used to be a callback, a paper application and a week of chasing collapses into a single unbroken conversation.

A win on both sides of the transaction

Risk mitigation, cost savings and a shorter path to coverage — for the person buying and for the carrier writing.

For the consumer

  • Answered immediately, at whatever hour they actually thought about it
  • Quoted honestly against the budget they named themselves
  • No pressure, no pitch that drifts, no callback that never comes
  • Covered and confirmed before the call ends

For the carrier

  • Lower acquisition cost with no floor to staff or scale
  • Identical disclosure on every single call
  • Cleaner applications and fewer downstream corrections
  • A complete, auditable record of every conversation
